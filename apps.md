# Apps

Self-hostable or local-first applications.

### [open-webui/open-webui](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui)
User-friendly AI interface. Supports Ollama, OpenAI-compatible APIs and more.
- **Stack**: Svelte

### [immich-app/immich](https://github.com/immich-app/immich)
High-performance self-hosted photo and video management.
- **Stack**: TypeScript

### [debpalash/VoiceStudio](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio)
Open-source, fully local ElevenLabs alternative: voice cloning, voice design, video dubbing, dictation, transcription and audiobook creation in 646 languages.
- **Stack**: Python
